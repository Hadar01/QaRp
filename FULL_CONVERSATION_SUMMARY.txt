--- KEY ARCHITECTURE: MCDropoutResNet50 ---

class MCDropoutResNet50(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dropout_rate=0.1):
        super().__init__()
        base = models.resnet50(weights='IMAGENET1K_V1')
        self.conv1 = base.conv1
        self.bn1 = base.bn1
        self.relu = base.relu
        self.maxpool = base.maxpool
        self.layer1 = base.layer1
        self.drop1 = nn.Dropout(p=dropout_rate)
        self.layer2 = base.layer2
        self.drop2 = nn.Dropout(p=dropout_rate)
        self.layer3 = base.layer3
        self.drop3 = nn.Dropout(p=dropout_rate)
        self.layer4 = base.layer4
        self.drop4 = nn.Dropout(p=dropout_rate)
        self.avgpool = base.avgpool
        self.drop_fc = nn.Dropout(p=dropout_rate)
        self.fc = nn.Linear(base.fc.in_features, 1)

    def forward(self, x):
        x = self.maxpool(self.relu(self.bn1(self.conv1(x))))
        x = self.drop1(self.layer1(x))
        x = self.drop2(self.layer2(x))
        x = self.drop3(self.layer3(x))
        x = self.drop4(self.layer4(x))
        x = self.avgpool(x)
        x = torch.flatten(x, 1)
        x = self.drop_fc(x)
        return self.fc(x)

7. IMPORTANT CONTEXT
================================================================

--- WHY RESNET-50 ---
ResNet-50 was chosen deliberately for comparability with Guo et al. 2017 and Ovadia et al. 2019 — the foundational calibration papers. This isolates the uncertainty method as the experimental variable. A ViT would confound architecture effects with method effects. This is a methodological choice to defend, not a weakness to apologize for.

--- CLASS-STRATIFIED ECE REVELATION ---
Overall ECE of 0.015 hides a 2x gap between positive (0.108) and negative (0.210) class calibration. Under noise S5, the gap explodes to 6.4x. The model is particularly dangerous for ruling OUT disease under shift — overconfidently saying "no effusion" when wrong. This is a major finding that emerged from the professor's feedback.

--- CONFORMAL EMPTY-SET vs INCORRECT ---
Two clinically distinct failure modes:
- Noise: 99.9% EMPTY sets — model refuses to predict (honest, detectable)
- Blur: 2.6% empty, 18.2% INCORRECT — model gives wrong answer (dangerous, silent)
Empty failures are safer because they can be automatically routed to human readers. Incorrect failures pass through the safety net silently.

--- MC DROPOUT MECHANISM ---
Last-layer dropout: Same features every pass, only varies final combination. Good under noise (features already destroyed), bad under blur (can't detect structural changes).
Full-network dropout: Different features every pass. Good under blur (feature diversity reveals uncertainty about structural changes), bad under noise (adds randomness on top of randomness).
The accuracy tradeoff (82.7% vs 90.9%) exists because full-network dropout forces FC to learn with randomly degraded features.
